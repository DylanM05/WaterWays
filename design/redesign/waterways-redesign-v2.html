WaterWays redesign, version 2

A quieter take on version 1, which stays as it was for comparison. Readings are sample data.

  • Cut the taglines, eyebrow labels and subtitles. Headings are one or two words.
  • One typeface on the website (IBM Plex Sans) and the iPhone’s own system font in the app.
  • Hairline borders and smaller corners; no shadows, glows, gradient fills or pill badges.
  • The staff gauge shows three numbers: the level now, and the 30-day high and low.
  • Saved stations and the province tiles keep their version 1 layout.
